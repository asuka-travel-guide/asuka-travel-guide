Visiting Asuka Historical National Government Park
Asuka Historical National Government Park sites are scattered throughout the village of Asuka. The village itself has been designated as a district whose historic character and cultural legacy are worthy of preservation, and the park is used by local residents for a broad range of activities. Please ensure that everyone can enjoy this resource by being respectful of others and exhibiting good manners during your time at the park.
