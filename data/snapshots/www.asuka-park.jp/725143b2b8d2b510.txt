Getting around the Asuka area
Asuka Aka Kame Tour Bus service, rental bicycles, and rental electric cars provide convenient options for getting around the Asuka area.
Asuka Aka Kame Tour Bus
The Asuka Tour Bus travels in a continuous circuit from Kintetsu Kashiharajingu-mae Station to Kintetsu Asuka Station, passing various famous sightseeing destinations in the village of Asuka on the way. The Tour Bus Day Pass provides a bargain-priced way to enjoy the Asuka area for a day.

Rental bicycles
As long as the weather’s good, there’s no better area to enjoy from the vantage point of a bicycle than the roads of Asuka. You’ll enjoy a leisurely trip back through the area’s ancient history, visiting sightseeing destinations as your fancy takes you. Fees, business hours, and the types of bicycles available vary with the business.
